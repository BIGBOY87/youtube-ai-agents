# Guardrails

This project is for legal organic YouTube growth only.

Allowed:
- Read your own channel metadata and analytics through OAuth.
- Generate SEO titles, descriptions, tags, hashtags and community post drafts.
- Generate replies to comments for manual review.
- Create growth recommendations based on CTR, views, retention and subscribers.

Not allowed:
- Fake views, fake subscribers or artificial engagement.
- Mass commenting, spam DMs or scraping users for unsolicited outreach.
- Credential sharing in chats or public repositories.
- Uploading or editing videos automatically without explicit owner review.
